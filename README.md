# 📊 Enterprise Agile Transformation & Velocity KPI Dashboard

An executive-level operational diagnostic framework mapping the alignment of cross-functional Scrum teams during a large-scale enterprise cloud migration ecosystem (SAP / Snowflake / Azure).

This framework showcases how to track a 10-day sprint cycle using multi-visualization metrics to identify systemic delivery bottlenecks—specifically tracking the financial and logistical impacts of **Multitasking Drag** and **Team Burnout Alerts**.

## 🚀 Business Case & Operational Risk Profile
When enterprises execute complex digital transformations, delivery cycles frequently stall due to unmanaged dependencies or bloated work pipelines. This project builds a centralized window of truth to audit operational metrics, moving teams away from "spreadsheet chaos" and toward locked-down executive dashboards.

## 🛠️ The Dataset (10-Day Sprint Trajectory)
The core simulation maps out an intensive 50-story-point delivery framework, tracking a team facing a major mid-sprint roadblock:
* **The Days 3–4 Plateau:** Points remaining stall flat at 38, signaling a complete halt in progress due to an unaddressed technical hurdle.
* **The Multitasking Drag Vector:** Visual proof showing active open tasks spiking to maximum thresholds (12 open items) precisely when velocity drops.
* **The Incomplete Work Gap:** A final 5-point gap on Day 10, highlighting a failure to meet sprint commitments and necessitating a capacity reset.

## 🎛️ Dashboard Architecture & Tooling Matrix
To align with standard enterprise data governance rules, this analytics stack is tiered by user intent:
1. **The Collaborative Engine (Google Sheets):** Leveraged cloud spreadsheets for real-time data input, conditional tracking formulas (`XLOOKUP`, `SUMIFS`), and automated risk alerts.
2. **The Executive Delivery Layer (Power BI / Tableau Connection):** Designed to connect directly to the spreadsheet backend via secure API cloud gateways or ODBC connectivity, enforcing strict data masking to prevent downstream data leakage.

## 💡 Strategic Takeaways for the C-Suite
* **Enforce WIP Limits:** Teams must finish open tasks before initiating new ones to eliminate structural processing drag.
* **Empirical Planning:** Adjust team capacity commitments for upcoming sprints down to match historical velocity bounds rather than forcing artificial overcommitment.
